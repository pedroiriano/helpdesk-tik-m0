Problem & supported use

From capture to coaching decision

Rim Finish keeps takeoff mechanics, flight window, ball protection, and landing context in one reviewable sequence.

Illustrative force and lift values require calibrated capture hardware before they can support training decisions. They are not medical measurements.

Pipeline outputs require deployment-specific calibration, tolerance, and operating-envelope validation.
