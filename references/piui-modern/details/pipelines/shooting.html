Problem & supported use

From capture to coaching decision

Shooting Lab organizes release, flight, and entry readings around the exact video frames that produced them. Coaches can inspect the evidence before accepting a cue.

The displayed values are illustrative. Production accuracy depends on camera placement, frame rate, lens profile, court calibration, and validation against the intended training environment.

Pipeline outputs require deployment-specific calibration, tolerance, and operating-envelope validation.

Prove the workflow before scale

  1. Calibrate rim, court lines, and camera perspective.
  2. Detect the gather, release, apex, and entry windows.
  3. Compare the trace with the athlete’s own validated baseline.
  4. Deliver one correction and verify it on the next rep.
